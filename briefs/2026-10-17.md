# Brief 2026-10-17 (Sat): Riftbound

**Story:** Riftbound: Radiance, the fifth set, launches Oct 23 with Pre-Rift events running Oct 16 to 22, and brings three new keywords (Deploy, Show Off, Disarm) and nine new Legends.

## Verified facts (2+ sources agree)
- Release Oct 23, 2026 (Riot's playriftbound.com Radiance overview; Star City Games; ICv2 sponsored post Sep 28; The Card Shop Finder Sep 29).
- Pre-Rift events Oct 16 to 22 (Riot overview, updated Oct 16; ICv2; Card Shop Finder).
- Nine new Champion Legends, each with an Overnumbered variant (Riot overview; ICv2; Star City Games/Card Shop Finder say 9 Legends and 180 cards incl. 60 Showcase).
- 180 new cards, 60+ Showcase (Star City Games; Card Shop Finder; Riot press release Mar 5 says 180-card pool, 60+ Showcase).
- New keywords: Deploy, Show Off, Disarm (Riot overview; Riot core rules patch notes, effective Oct 16).
- Products: Booster Display, Seraphine and Evelynn Showdown Decks, Vault (6 boosters, foil tokens, 36 Runes), Pre-Rift Kits (5 boosters + seeded pack), Heimerdinger, Academy Instructor promo at Pre-Rift events while supplies last (Riot overview; ICv2).
- Ultimate Rare: about 0.025% of packs, not guaranteed (Riot overview; ICv2).
- Named champions: Seraphine, Evelynn, Ekko headline; Ziggs, Jarvan IV also confirmed (Riot overview; Star City Games; Card Shop Finder).

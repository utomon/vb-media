# Brief 2026-10-09 (Fri): Pokémon TCG

**Story:** Mega Evolution—Delta Reign (ME06) arrives Nov 6, headlined by Mega Rayquaza ex and the new Legendary Stadium cards. Prerelease events start Oct 24.
No repeat: the Oct 8 Pokémon post covered October product dates only (Mini Tins, Deck Crafter's Collection, Battle Decks), not this set. Last 7 days of Metricool posts and briefs/ checked.
Note: the announcement itself is from Aug 20; it is chosen because prerelease is two weeks away and nothing newer was verifiable.

## Verified facts (2+ sources agree)
- Worldwide tabletop release Nov 6, 2026 (pokemon.com headline, PokeGuardian, PocketMonsters.Net, Pokeled).
- Headline cards: Mega Rayquaza ex (first Mega Rayquaza in the TCG since XY—Ancient Origins, per PocketMonsters.Net), Mega Golurk ex, Mega Malamar ex, Mega Golisopod ex.
- New "Legendary Stadium" mechanic: two Stadium cards pair up; 6 Stadiums make 3 Legendary Stadiums (Summit, Ocean Trench, Lava Lake per Pokeled); a pack with one half contains both (PocketMonsters.Net).
- US MSRP: booster pack $4.49 (10 cards), Elite Trainer Box $49.99, Pokémon Center ETB $59.99, 6-pack Booster Bundle $26.94, Build and Battle Box $21.99, 36-pack display $161.64 (PokeGuardian, Pokeled agree).
- Pokémon TCG Live release Nov 5 (PocketMonsters.Net, Pokeled).
- Prerelease: Play! Pokémon events from Oct 24 (PocketMonsters.Net, Pokeled; Pokeled says through Nov 1).

# Brief 2026-10-13 (Tue): One Piece Card Game

**Story:** Dracule Mihawk (OP14-020) is now banned in the One Piece Card Game as of Oct 12, with a replacement Leader, P-163 Dracule Mihawk, coming through future events.
No repeat: Metricool last 7 days (Pokémon Oct releases, YGO Beyond the Brave, MTG Star Trek x2, Pokémon Delta Reign, Lorcana Hyperia City, 3D app post, Pokémon price movers) and briefs/ checked; no One Piece post or brief.

## Verified facts (2+ sources agree)
- Effective Oct 12, 2026: OP14-020 Dracule Mihawk is banned, parallel (alt-art) versions included (official site; onepiece.gg; The Card Shop Finder).
- Reason given by the developers: certain combinations became stronger than intended and it heavily limits viable {Slash} Leaders (official site; Card Shop Finder).
- No cards were restricted or unrestricted in this announcement (official site; onepiece.gg).
- A new Leader, P-163 Dracule Mihawk, is to be distributed at future events (official site; onepiece.gg; Card Shop Finder). Dates/event details not stated.
- Bonus (official June 18 presentation, plus Samurai Sword Tokyo): Extra Booster EB-05 (ONE PIECE Heroines Edition vol. 2) releases Oct 30 in English. US pack MSRP $4.99 is from one retailer blog only (reported; omit from post).

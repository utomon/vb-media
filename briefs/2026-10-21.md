## Verified facts (2+ sources agree)
- Release date Nov 13, 2026 (Wizards preview page; WPN product page; Bleeding Cool Oct 20; CGMagazine).
- Mechanics confirmed by CGMagazine (Jul 14 First Look) and Bleeding Cool (Oct 20), with MTG Rocks also covering the first three:
  - Assimilate: brings a creature under your control with a +1/+1 counter, and it becomes a Borg artifact creature.
  - Face a Dilemma: triggers when you choose modes for a modal spell or ability.
  - Federation: ability word that counts different creature types among your non-Borg creatures.
  - Spacecraft (with Station/Stationing): returning from Edge of Eternities, represents the starships.

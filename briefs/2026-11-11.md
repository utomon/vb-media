## Verified facts (official pages + 2 independent sources)
- Arena release: Nov 10, 2026 (Wizards Arena announcements Nov 9 "comes to MTG Arena tomorrow, November 10"; Vice Nov 10; MTG Rocks).
- Tabletop release: Nov 13 (Wizards Star Trek product page "Release date: November 13"; Vice; MTG Rocks). Prerelease events Nov 6 to 12 at local game stores (MTG Rocks; Wizards prerelease guide Nov 6).
- Arena Direct (Star Trek Sealed, Best-of-One): Nov 13 to 22; 7 wins earn a Star Trek Collector Booster box while supplies last (Wizards Arena page; Vice).
- MagicCon: Atlanta, Nov 13 to 15, last MagicCon of 2026; World Championship 32 uses Standard Constructed and Star Trek Draft (Wizards Arena page).
- Products at launch include Play Boosters, Collector Boosters, Draft Night, Bundle, Beam Me Up Bundle, four Commander Decks, Scene Boxes (Wizards product page). Four Commander decks: Federation Fleet, Landing Party, Klingon Fury, We Are The Borg (MTG Rocks).
- Draws on about 60 years of Star Trek history (Wizards product page).

# Brief 2026-10-29 (Thu): Yu-Gi-Oh! TCG

**Story:** Konami's first Yu-Gi-Oh! TCG set of 2027, Immortal Phoenix, lands Jan 29, 2027 (North America) with 100 cards built around 10 new Sun God cards, including a new Winged Dragon of Ra Fusion, plus a new Xyz archetype, Celestigard.
No repeat: briefs/ and Metricool Oct 20 to Nov 5 checked. YGO posts so far: Beyond the Brave (Oct 8), Magnificent Maestros (Oct 15), banlist (Oct 22). Nothing on Immortal Phoenix. Note: the set was first announced Sept 22 (ICv2); ICv2 put it in its 2027 calendar on Oct 27 and no fresher verified YGO news turned up this run. Use as "next up" framing, not "just revealed".

## Verified facts (2+ sources agree)
- Name: Immortal Phoenix (IMPH), a booster set (Konami official; YuGiOhMeta; ICv2).
- Release: Jan 29, 2027 in North America (Konami US page lists launch and Tournament Legal date 01/29/2027; Official Tournament Store launch 01/27/2027; ICv2 and YuGiOhMeta agree on Jan 29). Konami EU page lists Jan 28, 2027, so date varies by region.
- 100 cards: 10 Secret Rares, 14 Ultra Rares, 26 Super Rares, 50 Commons. Also 5 cards as extended-art Ultra Rares or Starlight Rares and 20 more as Starlight Rares (Konami US and EU pages, YuGiOhMeta, ICv2).
- Centers on 10 new Sun God cards; The Immortal Sun God lets you summon The Winged Dragon of Ra, and The Winged Dragon of Ra, Sun God of Darkness is a Fusion Monster in the set (Konami US page, YuGiOhMeta, ICv2 wording differs slightly).
- Also supports Salamangreat and Winter Raven / Sinful Spoils / Diabellze, and debuts the Celestigard archetype (Star-Eclipsing Aetos Dios, Rank 5) plus Fusion of Above and Below and Chomplush (Konami US and EU, YuGiOhMeta).
- Pack price $4.49 (ICv2 only, so "reported"; not on Konami page).

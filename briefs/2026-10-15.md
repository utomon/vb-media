**Story:** Magnificent Maestros (Nov 13) puts 18 re-imagined anime-rival cards in serial-numbered Grand Master Rares, only 100 copies of each (1,800 total).

## Verified facts (2+ sources agree)
- Release: Nov 13, 2026 (ICv2, Aug 7; Asmodee/ICv2 calendar listing). Konami's EU product page says Nov 12 for Europe. Say "Nov 13 (EU Nov 12)".
- 18 re-imagined cards based on anime characters: three each for Kaiba, Zane Truesdale, Jack Atlas, Kite Tenjo, Yuto/Yugo/Yuri and Varis (Konami EU page; ICv2).
- Those 18 also exist as Grand Master Rares, serial-numbered 001 to 100 (Konami EU page; ICv2). 18 x 100 = 1,800 copies in total is the Konami page's figure.
- 21 new cards for Toon, Witchcrafter and Unchained (Konami EU page; ICv2).
- 24 Extended Art cards; each box holds three 5-card packs and 3 Secret Rares that can upgrade to Starlight Rares (Konami EU page; ICv2).

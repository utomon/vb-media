- TCGplayer's blog gives no card number. Supreme Victors has three Garchomp cards: #5/147 Rare Holo, #60/147 Uncommon (Garchomp C), #145/147 Garchomp C LV.X.
- Price match points to #5 Holofoil: MyDex TCG (quoting TCGplayer) Holofoil market $102.42; PokeScope (TCGPlayer data) Holofoil $103.47; both near the blog's $97.03 (Oct 7). #60 is ~$6 to $11, so it cannot be the card. #145 LV.X lists $54.71 to $217.12 across sites and PokeScope shows no 30-day history, so it is less likely but not strictly excluded.

- Stamped variants of #5 exist (League, Staff) and are worth far more (PokeScope: League $140, Staff $549); the slide must say regular Holo, unstamped.
- TCGplayer's own product page could not be read (needs JavaScript), so this is an inference from price match, not a direct TCGplayer confirmation. Label: "Garchomp #5/147 Holo (per TCGplayer pricing)".

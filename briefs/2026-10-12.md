# Brief 2026-10-12 (Mon): Pokémon market movers, older cards climbing

**Story:** Older Pokémon cards are climbing in price. Gold Star Rayquaza is up on two independent trackers, and TCGplayer's 30-day report shows sell-outs pushing Garchomp, Zoroark and Pikachu W Stamped higher, with the 30th Celebration cited as one driver.
No repeat: Metricool last 7 days (Pokémon Oct releases, YGO Beyond the Brave, MTG Star Trek x2, Pokémon Delta Reign, Lorcana Hyperia City, 3D app post Oct 11) and briefs/ checked. No price-movement post yet.
Note: no price DECREASES were found in any source (CardValue lists no fallers). Post is "rising" only; do not claim a market-wide trend.

## Verified facts
Two-source (independent data):
- Rayquaza ☆ (Gold Star, EX Deoxys): CardValue (live data, page dated Oct 12) shows ~$2,503.54 Near Mint, +180.8% over 7 days, #1 weekly gainer. CardCenteringTool (Midpoint index, Oct 5) lists Gold Star Rayquaza graded up ~30% month-over-month, trading near $3,000. Direction agrees; size differs (raw 7-day vs graded 30-day), so quote each with its own window and source, never merge.
- Common driver language: 30th anniversary interest and thin supply. CardCenteringTool: 30th Celebration Charizard PSA 10 up ~25% MoM (~$2,400, approximate). TCGplayer links Pikachu W Stamped to the 30th Celebration redraw of Jungle Pikachu.
One-source (TCGplayer seller blog, Oct 7, Near Mint sales Sep 5 to Oct 4, 30 days; it states dollar changes only, percentages below are my own calculation):
- Garchomp (Supreme Victors): +$51.11 to $97.03 (~111%); a buyer took the last four NM copies on Oct 2. CARD IDENTIFIED (verified Oct 12, see below): Garchomp #5/147 Rare Holo (Platinum: Supreme Victors), regular Holofoil, RAW Near Mint.
- Zoroark (BW19 promo): +$20.99 to $28.34 (~286%); NM listings under $20 sold out in September.
- Pikachu [W Stamped] (WoTC promo): +$71.50 to $248.26 (~40%).
- Kyogre-EX (Team Aqua, Double Crisis): +$56.39 to $576.61 (~11%).
- Gengar & Mimikyu GX (Secret): +$73.29 to $546.23 (~15%).
- Reasons are TCGplayer's reading ("likely reason"), not confirmed causes. Use "reportedly" / "per TCGplayer".

## Garchomp card check (Oct 12)
- TCGplayer's blog gives no card number. Supreme Victors has three Garchomp cards: #5/147 Rare Holo, #60/147 Uncommon (Garchomp C), #145/147 Garchomp C LV.X.
- Price match points to #5 Holofoil: MyDex TCG (quoting TCGplayer) Holofoil market $102.42; PokeScope (TCGPlayer data) Holofoil $103.47; both near the blog's $97.03 (Oct 7). #60 is ~$6 to $11, so it cannot be the card. #145 LV.X lists $54.71 to $217.12 across sites and PokeScope shows no 30-day history, so it is less likely but not strictly excluded.
- Direction cross-check: CardTrack shows Garchomp #5/147 raw Near Mint $50 with +44% (30-day vs 90-day median sale price). Different method and price level, same direction (up).
- Stamped variants of #5 exist (League, Staff) and are worth far more (PokeScope: League $140, Staff $549); the slide must say regular Holo, unstamped.
- TCGplayer's own product page could not be read (needs JavaScript), so this is an inference from price match, not a direct TCGplayer confirmation. Label: "Garchomp #5/147 Holo (per TCGplayer pricing)".
- Aggregator prices differ by site and date; use the blog's $97.03 and +$51.11 as the stated figure.

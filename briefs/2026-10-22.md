# Brief 2026-10-22 (Thu): Yu-Gi-Oh! TCG

**Story:** The biggest TCG Forbidden & Limited List update in years went live Oct 19: 34 cards newly Forbidden (including Called by the Grave and Red-Eyes Dark Dragoon), 8 Limited, plus Droll & Lock Bird cut to 1 copy. Konami revealed it in five parts during YCS Houston (Oct 16 to 18).
No repeat: Metricool Oct 15 to 30 and briefs/ checked. YGO posts so far: Beyond the Brave launch (Oct 8) and Magnificent Maestros (Oct 15). Nothing on the banlist.

## Verified facts (2+ sources agree)
- Effective date: Oct 19, 2026 (YGOrganization final list; YuGiOhMeta parts 1 and 3; Polkastarter).
- Revealed in five parts around YCS Houston, Oct 16 to 18 (Polkastarter; YuGiOhMeta "Part x/5" series; Konami blog series "Part 1/2/5" exists, page itself blocked in this run).
- Counts: 34 cards newly Forbidden and 8 newly Limited (YGOrganization counted from its list; Polkastarter and YuGiOhMeta overview agree on 34 and 8). YGOrganization also lists 1 Semi-Limited: Sky Striker Mobilize - Engage! (Polkastarter agrees).
- Forbidden includes Called by the Grave, Red-Eyes Dark Dragoon, Albion the Branded Dragon, Varudras the Final Bringer of the End Times (YGOrganization; Polkastarter for the first two). Amorphage Goliath and Amorphage Sloth also go 3 to 0 (YuGiOhMeta part 1; AllThingsMisplay).
- Droll & Lock Bird goes from 2 copies to 1 (YuGiOhMeta, YGOrganization, Polkastarter).
- Perfectron Hydradrive Dragon debuts at Limited (YuGiOhMeta, YGOrganization, Polkastarter). Polkastarter says it joins the list on Nov 11 on release in Magnificent Maestros; treat the exact date as reported.

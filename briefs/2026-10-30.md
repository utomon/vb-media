# Brief 2026-10-30 (Fri): Pokémon TCG
**Story:** McDonald's and Pokémon announced (Oct 29) a 30th-anniversary Happy Meal: 15 mini Happy-Meal-box toys plus a separate 15-card Pokémon TCG set, starting Nov 10 in the US, with other countries rolling out Nov 2026 to Feb 2027.
No repeat: Metricool Oct 23 to Nov 6 checked (Deck Crafter's Oct 23, Pocket Dual Fusion Oct 26, no McDonald's post) and briefs/ checked (no McDonald's story). Note: this is a promo set, separate from the 30th Celebration expansion.

## Verified facts (2+ sources agree)
- US launch Tuesday Nov 10, 2026 at participating restaurants, while supplies last (Essential Japan, Dexerto, The Main Street Mouse, Joseph Writer Anderson).
- 15 collectible toys, each a mini Happy Meal box themed on one Pokémon, and 15 collectible trading cards (all four sources).
- Announced Oct 29 as part of Pokémon's 30th anniversary (Essential Japan, Main Street Mouse; Dexerto cites McDonald's official Happy Meal Instagram).
- Toy Pokémon confirmed in at least 2 sources: Pikachu, Eevee, Charmander, Squirtle, Umbreon.
- Rollout: Nov 2026 US, Australia, Canada, Fiji, France, New Zealand, Spain; dozens more countries Dec 2026 and Jan 2027; Ireland and UK Feb 2027 (Essential Japan, Dexerto). Exact non-US dates not announced.

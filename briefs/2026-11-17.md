# Brief 2026-11-17 (Tue): One Piece Card Game

**Story:** OP-18 "The Dominance of God" launches in English this Friday, Nov 20 ($4.99 packs), and the official page now names its chase cards: Super Alt Art Nico Robin, Super Leader Alt Art Saint Gunko, an Yoshitaka Amano alt art Saint Shamrock, and 4th Anniversary Holy Knights alt arts.

No repeat (partly): briefs/ Nov 3 to Nov 16 checked. OP-18 was briefed and posted Oct 27 as a reveal (release date, price, Water Seven theme, Franky Leader, anniversary event). That post did NOT name the chase cards, and the Amano card was marked unconfirmed. Today's angle is "launches Friday + the chase cards are confirmed", so lead on the chase cards, do not re-run the Oct 27 release-date slide. Earlier One Piece briefs: ST-37/38 (Nov 3), OP-19/EB-06/PEB-01 roadmap (Nov 10).
Alternative if David wants a different set: ST-37/ST-38 release event (official event page, Dec 18 to Jan 10), but it is minor news.

## Verified facts (official OP-18 page + official event/news list + Vice)
- English release Nov 20, 2026; Japan Nov 21 (official OP-18 page, updated Nov 6/7; Vice Nov 11).
- MSRP $4.99 per pack; 127+1 card types (official page).
- Theme: Water Seven and the Holy Knights of God; Saint Gunko and Saint Shamrock debut in the card game (official page; Vice).
- Official page lists six new Leaders: Karoo, Monkey.D.Luffy, Ms. All Sunday, Franky, Saint Gunko, Spandam.
- Chase cards named on the official page: Super Alt Art Nico Robin (OP18-031); Super Leader Alt Art Saint Gunko (OP18-060); Yoshitaka Amano original-art alt art Saint Shamrock (OP18-119); 4th Anniversary alt art Saint Gunko (OP18-065). Vice describes Nico Robin as an expected chase card (its opinion).
- Card list for OP-18 went live on the official site Nov 13 (official news list).
- Tournament legal 7 days after release (official page, Vice).
- 4th Anniversary release event in stores Nov 20 to Dec 17, P-161 Monkey.D.Luffy promo (from the Oct 27 brief, official event page; re-check before use).

# Brief 2026-11-04 (Wed): Magic: The Gathering

**Story:** Wizards revealed the Extra Life 2026 Secret Lair "Rise of the Polygons" on Nov 3: a five-card, 1990s 64-bit-style drop (Balefire Dragon, Big Score, Young Pyromancer, Sword of Truth and Justice, Elemental Token) on sale Nov 16 to 30, with 50% of the price going to charity.

## Verified facts (official page + 3 independent sources agree)
- Revealed Nov 3, 2026 (Wizards "Magic Levels Up with Extra Life for 2026"; Bleeding Cool, Star City Games and Vice all dated Nov 3).
- Drop: Secret Lair "Rise of the Polygons | Extra Life 2026", five items, 1 each: Balefire Dragon, Big Score, Young Pyromancer, Sword of Truth and Justice, Elemental Token (all four sources).
- Theme: late-1990s 64-bit / low-poly adventure game look (Bleeding Cool, Vice).
- Price: $39.99 non-foil, $49.99 foil (all four sources).
- Sale window: Nov 16 to Nov 30, 2026 (all four sources); sold at MagicSecretLair.com (Wizards page). Limited quantities at the MagicCon: Atlanta merch booth, Nov 13 to 15 (Wizards page, Vice).
- Charity: 50% of the sales price (less third-party transaction fees) goes to Children's Miracle Network via Our Change Foundation, supporting Extra Life and Seattle Children's Hospital (Wizards page footnote, Vice, Bleeding Cool).
- MTG Arena: Extra Life Secret Lair Sleeve Bundle 3,000 gems, Sleeve Bundle 1,800 gems, Sticker Emote 800 gems, Nov 16 to 30 (Wizards page, Star City Games).

## Uncertain / "reported"

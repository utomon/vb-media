# Brief 2026-11-03 (Tue): One Piece Card Game

**Story:** Two new English Starter Decks land Dec 18, 2026: ST-37 The Desert Princess and the Pirates (Straw Hat Crew and Alabasta) and ST-38 Baroque Works, $16.99 each, 51 cards plus 10 DON!! and a 3-card bonus pack.

## Verified facts (official pages + independent source)
- ST-37 STARTER DECK -The Desert Princess and the Pirates- and ST-38 STARTER DECK -Baroque Works-: English release Dec 18, 2026 (both official product pages; EVORETRO retailer blog Oct 25 agrees).
- MSRP $16.99 each (both official pages; EVORETRO).
- Contents per deck: 51-card constructed deck, 10 DON!! cards, 1 bonus pack (3 cards), 1 playsheet. Rarity: 1 Leader, 2 Super Rare, 14 Common (official pages).
- ST-37: featuring Luffy and characters from the Straw Hat Crew and the Kingdom of Alabasta (official page).
- ST-38: Blue/Black deck that cycles your hand and KOs opposing characters (official page); members of Baroque Works.
- Japan release: Dec 19, 2026 (Samurai Sword Tokyo Oct 21; OneMall Nov 2 agree).

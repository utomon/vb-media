## Verified facts (3+ independent sources agree on date, price and contents)
- Release: January 15, 2027 (US) (ICv2 Oct 30; PokeGuardian Oct 29; Essential Japan Oct 29; PocketMonsters.net Oct 30; Joseph Writer Anderson Oct 29).
- Price: $14.99 MSRP (ICv2, PokeGuardian, PocketMonsters.net, Joseph Writer Anderson). Essential Japan does not state a price.
- Contents: 20 Collect & Share booster packs (3 cards each, so 60 cards, per ICv2), 2 sheets of To/From label stickers, 3 sheets of decorating stickers (5 in total), and packaging that converts into a mailbox for receiving valentines (all sources).
- Billed as the first Valentine's Day TCG product (ICv2, Essential Japan headline "first-ever").
- Card lineup, artwork and any stamp are not revealed yet (PokeGuardian, Joseph Writer Anderson).

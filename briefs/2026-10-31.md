## Verified facts
- Name/code: Digimon Card Game Booster IGNITION OF X [BT-27] (official en.digimoncard.com product page; Official Digimon EN X account; withthewill.net).
- Theme: X Antibody Digimon, with support for EXTRA BOOSTER CHIVALROUS XIII [EX-13] (Official Digimon EN X post, snippet; official world news report: "X Antibody").
- 108 card types: 40 Common, 26 Uncommon, 21 Rare, 12 Super Rare, 3 Ultimate Rare, 2 Secret Rare, 4 Special; parallel rares included (official en.digimoncard.com page).
- Release: official English product page lists Dec 5, 2026 (this matches the Japan date per withthewill.net). Official EN X account says "In stores, December, 2026". US prerelease Dec 4 and US retail Dec 11 per Zeltrix (retailer) and withthewill.net. Use "December 2026" plus "Dec 11 in the US (reported)".
- Companion starters ST-25 Alysion Rubeusdramon and ST-26 Alysion Yukinamon: official page lists Dec 5, 2026; withthewill.net says Dec 4 in the US. Each includes a BT-27 booster pack (withthewill.net only, "reported").
- Next: EX-14 Malevolent VII (Seven Great Demon Lords) is scheduled for January 2027 (official world news report; official products page).

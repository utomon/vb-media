# Brief 2026-10-08 (Thu): Yu-Gi-Oh! TCG

**Story:** Beyond the Brave, the Joey Wheeler-themed Core Booster, launches in the US on Oct 9 (EU/OC Oct 8).
No repeat: recent Metricool posts are Pokemon (Oct releases) and MTG Star Trek.

## Verified facts
- Official page: launch 10/09/2026, Official Tournament Store launch 10/07, tournament legal 10/09; booster pack MSRP $4.49; set size 100 (contents subject to change).
- Rarity line per official page: 10 Secret, 14 Ultra, 26 Super, 50 Common; 5 cards with extended-art Ultra and Starlight Rares plus 20 more Starlight Rares.
- Theme: Joey Wheeler (new takes on his cards around Dark Time Wizard), Destiny HERO for Aster Phoenix, Atlantis/Daedalus sea theme, Ashtra Trap-based monsters, Audhumla, Progenitor of the Frozen Expanse (called a world premiere card).
- Yugipedia: second Core Booster of Series 14 after Chaos Origins; first Core Booster to feature an anime character other than Yami Yugi (Joey); English NA Oct 9, EU/OC Oct 8.
- ICv2 release calendar lists Oct 9, 2026 for Beyond the Brave.

## Uncertain / label "reported"
- Cards per pack (9) and packs per box (24) confirmed from the pack and box images David supplied (Konami packaging).
- A retailer article (thecardshopfinder.com) says $4.99 per pack; the official page says $4.49. Use $4.49 or leave price out.
- Tournament stores may get stock a couple of days early.

# Brief 2026-10-20 (Tue): One Piece Card Game

**Story:** EXTRA BOOSTER ONE PIECE Heroines Edition vol.2 (EB-05) arrives Oct 30 in English, headlined by a new Nico Robin Leader (EB05-010) and 75+2 card types.
No repeat: Metricool Oct 13 to 25 (One Piece Mihawk ban Oct 13, MTG Marvel Oct 14, Riftbound Radiance Oct 17) and briefs/ Oct 10 to 19 checked. The Oct 13 brief mentioned EB-05 only as a "bonus" fact and it was not posted. Not a repeat of the Mihawk ban story.

## Verified facts (official page + second source)
- English release date: Oct 30, 2026 (official EB-05 product page; Samurai Sword Tokyo cites the official English site). Japan's release is Oct 31 (SST, citing the Japanese product page).
- Pack MSRP US $4.99 (official English product page; SST agrees on $4.99).
- Set size: 75+2 card types (official page; SST citing official JP page).
- New Leader: Nico Robin, EB05-010 (official page; SST gives the same number from reveal images).
- Official page lists special SP-design cards for returning heroines (examples named: EB05-006 Miss Buckingham Stussy, OP14-033 Perona, ST17-004 Boa Hancock) and an exclusive alt-art of EB05-055 illustrated by Kentaro Yabuki.
- Tournament legal 7 days after release (official page).

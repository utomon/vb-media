## Verified facts (official product page + official Konami blog + 3 independent sources)
- Launch Nov 13, 2026; official tournament store launch Nov 11; Konami tournament legal date Nov 13 (official product page yugioh-card.com/en/products/mams; Konami blog Nov 10; ICv2 Aug 7; Bleeding Cool Oct 18; Cardsrealm Aug 7).
- 125-card set; each box has 3 packs of 5 cards plus 3 Secret Rares that can upgrade to Starlight Rares (official page).
- MSRP $34.99 per box (official page; Bleeding Cool says $35).
- 18 reimagined anime-inspired cards (Kaiba, Zane, Kite, Yuri/Yugo/Yuto, Varis), 21 new Toon, Witchcrafter and Unchained cards, 10 more anime-based new cards, 10 chibi variant art cards, 24 extended art cards, 40 foil upgrades (official page).
- Grand Master Rares: only the 18 reimagined cards, serial-numbered 001 to 100, 100 copies each for the Americas and 100 for Europe-distributed markets, 1,800 total (official page).
- Genesys points update published Nov 10: 23 new MAMS cards get initial points so they can be used at events this weekend. Highest: Dark Rebellion Xyz Dragon, Four Heavenly Dragons 80; Gatling Ogre 51; Mind Scan 50; Demiurge Ema 33. Lowest: Hyper Rank-Up-Magic Ultimate Force 1; Witchcrafter Celebration 5; Witchcrafter Distortion 7 (Konami Yu-Gi-Oh! blog, official; not found elsewhere).

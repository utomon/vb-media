## Verified facts (2+ sources agree)
- Release Nov 6, 2026 for the Day and Night Ultra-Premium Collections, the Ditto Premium Collection and the Mew / Mewtwo Figure Collections (Rare Candy dated Sep 2; Sawadeekard dated Aug 6; House of Carts updated Sep 27; GosuGamers says "November").
- Ultra-Premium Collection (Day = Espeon ex, Night = Umbreon ex): 29 booster packs of the 30th Celebration expansion plus 1 Classic Collection pack, foil Pikachu ex promo, foil Espeon ex or Umbreon ex promo, playmat, 65 sleeves, deck box, metal coin, 6 damage-counter dice (ICv2 Jul 9; Rare Candy; Sawadeekard; GosuGamers).
- Ditto Premium Collection: 8 packs, Ditto promo, acrylic display (Rare Candy; Sawadeekard; House of Carts; GosuGamers).
- Mew and Mewtwo Figure Collections: 5 packs, foil promo, oversize card, sculpted figure; sold separately per Pokémon (Rare Candy; Sawadeekard; House of Carts; GosuGamers).
- Next wave: Dec 4 Binder Collection and Sylveon ex / Greninja ex Tins. The Dec 4 tins are retailer-reported (first-party US confirmation pending per House of Carts). Leave out of the post.

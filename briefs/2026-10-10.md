# Brief 2026-10-10 (Sat): Disney Lorcana

**Story:** Hyperia City (Set 14) prerelease starts Oct 16 and it launches everywhere Oct 23; new "ink drops" mechanic and first Coco glimmers.
No repeat: last 7 days of Metricool posts (Pokemon Oct releases, YGO Beyond the Brave, MTG Star Trek x2, Pokemon Delta Reign) and briefs/ checked; no Lorcana. Other Sat candidates (Riftbound Radiance Oct 23) only had one source this run.

## Verified facts (2+ sources agree)
- Prerelease Oct 16, 2026 (game stores, select Disney Parks/stores); general release Oct 23, 2026 everywhere (official Lorcana press release + product page; CGMagazine; Zatu).
- New mechanic: ink drops, one-time-use ink counters spent to play cards or power up others (official press release; CGMagazine; Zatu). Ink drops do not reset between turns (CGMagazine only, leave out).
- Setting: Hyperia City, metropolis of magic, tech and inks; Pixar's Coco glimmers (Miguel, Hector) (ICv2 Aug 5; Zatu; official D23 recap via Disney Parks Blog mentions Hector Rivera card).
- Two Iconic cards: Mickey Mouse (Best In Town) and Cinderella (Unintentional Icon) (CGMagazine, Zatu).
- Products on official product page: Prerelease Boxes, Illumineer's Trove (8 boosters + 6 damage dice), booster display and packs.

## Uncertain / label "reported"
- US prices (display $143.76, Trove $49.99, prerelease box $39.99) from ICv2 only (Aug 5): say "reported" or omit. Official pages state no prices.
- Set card count not stated. Collector Boosters arrive with Into the Inkdark (2027), not Hyperia City.
- Announcement is from Aug-Sep; chosen because prerelease is 6 days away.

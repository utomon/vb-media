## Verified facts (3+ sources agree; official page blocked)
- Launch: 6 PM PT on Oct 28, which is Oct 29 in many regions (Insider Gaming time table; Miketendo64: Oct 28 6 PM PDT = Oct 29 01:00 UK; Gaming on Phone: Oct 28; iPhone in Canada says Oct 29, a time-zone effect). Jakarta: about 08:00 WIB Oct 29.
- First dual-type Pokémon cards in Pocket: Mega Mewtwo X ex (Psychic + Fighting), Mega Greninja ex (Water + Darkness) (iPhone in Canada, Nintendojo, Gaming on Phone, Miketendo64, Pokémon Zone).
- Also featured: Pikachu ex, Mew ex, Mega Dragonite ex, new-art Venusaur, Charizard, Blastoise (Miketendo64, Nintendojo, iPhone in Canada).
- Six rental decks available until Dec 15, 2026; Dragon-type Elite Deck with Mega Rayquaza ex via Gifts (Gaming on Phone, Miketendo64).
- Special Pack: First Partners runs Oct 31 to Dec 26 (iPhone in Canada, Miketendo64, Gaming on Phone). New sets will come about every 6 weeks instead of 4.

# Brief 2026-11-18 (Wed): Magic: The Gathering

**Story:** Wizards revealed the product line for Nauctis: The Sunken Realm, Magic's first underwater plane and first set of 2027: tabletop Feb 5, 2027 (Arena Feb 2), headlined by Abyssal Lotus, an underwater take on Black Lotus. Preorders are open.

## Verified facts (Wizards product page + ICv2 + Vice agree)
- Nauctis: The Sunken Realm is the first Magic booster set of 2027 and "Magic's first ever underwater plane" (Wizards product page; ICv2 and Vice dated Nov 16).
- Tabletop release Feb 5, 2027 (Wizards, ICv2, Vice). Arena release Feb 2 (Vice). Prereleases Jan 29 to Feb 4 (Vice; not listed on the Wizards page).
- Standard rotation happens Jan 29 (Vice).
- Products: Booster Packs, Collector Boosters, Bundle, Secret Lair Bundle, Draft Night, Commander Decks plus Premium Edition (foil) versions (Wizards, ICv2).
- Preorders are open via the Wizards page retailer links (Wizards).
- Abyssal Lotus is the chase card: underwater Black Lotus take with a Descent mechanic (ICv2; Wizards names it as a Collector Booster chase card).
- Story begins Jan 4, 2027 with reveals/spoilers expected (Vice).

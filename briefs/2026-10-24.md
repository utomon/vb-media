## Verified facts (official page + 3 independent sources)
- Release Oct 30, 2026 (official gundam-gcg.com GD06 page; ICv2 Oct 9; Dork Sep 23; GundamLab; tcgindex).
- Features cards from Mobile Suit Gundam ZZ and Mobile Suit Gundam 0083: Stardust Memory, both making their card game debut (official page; ICv2 says "card game debuts"; Dork; GundamLab).
- 135 cards plus 8 token types: 50 Common, 36 Uncommon, 32 Rare, 12 Legend Rare, 5 Special, 8 Token (official page; ICv2).
- 12 cards per pack, MSRP $4.99 per pack in the US (official page; ICv2; Dork). UK RRP £3.75 per GundamLab (retailer, not used).
- Chase cards named by GundamLab only (retailer blog): ZZ Gundam LR and GP01Fb "Zephyranthes" Full Burnern. Label "reported"; not used in the caption.

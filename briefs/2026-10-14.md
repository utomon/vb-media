# Brief 2026-10-14 (Wed): Magic: The Gathering

**Story:** Wizards announced Marvel: Darkhold Destiny (a Universes Beyond set with an original dimension-hopping story) for April 9, 2027, with preorders already open.
No repeat: Metricool last 7 days and briefs/ checked; MTG posts so far were Star Trek release dates only. Banned and Restricted (Oct 12) was considered and rejected: it only changed Arena's Competitive Brawl, and sources disagree on which cards were banned outright vs narrowed to commander-only.

## Verified facts (2+ sources agree)
- Release date is April 9, 2027 (Wizards announcement page; Wizards product page; Wargamer; Games.gg).
- Announced at New York Comic Con, announcement dated Oct 10, 2026 (Wizards; Wargamer; Games.gg).
- First Universes Beyond set where Wizards tells an original story with Marvel: dimension-hopping plot through the Dark Dimension and Marvel's gods (Wizards; Wargamer).
- A new Marvel Comics story by Jackson Lanzing and Collin Kelly introduces an original character: digital Feb 2027, print April 2027 (Wizards; Wargamer; Games.gg).
- Teased/confirmed characters include Doctor Strange, Scarlet Witch, Blade, Moon Knight, Hela, Sif, Dormammu and Magik (Wargamer; Wizards confirms Magik and "major heroes and villains"). Use only characters on the slide that both sources name: Magik, Doctor Strange, Scarlet Witch.
- Four Commander decks: Lords of Hel, Gods of Asgard, Strange Academy, Midnight Sons (Wargamer; Games.gg).
- Product line includes Play Boosters, Collector Boosters, Bundle, Arcanum Bundle, Draft Night, two Scene Boxes (Assault on Asgard, Skirmish at the Sanctum) and Collector's Edition Commander decks (Wizards product page; Games.gg).
- Preorders open now (Wizards announcement; Games.gg).

# Brief 2026-11-14 (Sat): Riftbound (League of Legends TCG)

**Story:** Riot banned 8 cards and 2 battlefields in Riftbound Standard and 2v2 Constructed, effective Monday Nov 23, after Riven, Broken Blade dominated the European Regional Championship in Stuttgart.
No repeat: briefs/ Nov 1 to Nov 13 checked. Riftbound was briefed only for Radiance (Oct 17/18) and The Reckoning release (Nov 7). This ban announcement was never briefed. Skipped for Saturday: Gundam GD07 Blazing Fist (revealed Jul 29, too old), Lorcana Into the Inkdark preorder/serialized cards (Oct 23 to 25, too old), Digimon BT-27/BT-28 (already briefed Oct 31). ## Verified facts (official Riot page + Star City Games + The Gamer + Riftbound.gg, all published Nov 13)
- Announcement date: Nov 13, 2026, by Riot (game director Dave Guskin). Title: "Radiance Ban List Updates (Effective November 23, 2026)".
- Effective Monday Nov 23, 2026, Standard and 2v2 Constructed. Events before then can still use the cards.
- 10 bans in total, 8 cards: Riven, Broken Blade; Zhonya's Hourglass; Stellacorn Herder; Nocturne, Horrifying; Rhasa the Sunderer; Brynhir Thundersong; Dazzling Aurora; Baited Hook.
- 2 battlefields: Emperor's Dais; Star Spring.
- Official page: Riven made up 43% of European Regional Championship decks and won about 60% against the field (Riot's numbers). The Gamer adds Riven was reportedly about half of top-eight decks.
- Riot said the design team avoids bans within two weeks of premier events; future ban announcements come on the Friday two weeks before each Continental and the Friday two weeks after (Star City Games).
- Not in force for Korea and Traditional Chinese regions until Radiance releases there (official page, Star City Games).
- Baited Hook was banned ahead of the January World Showcase because upcoming sets could make it a problem (official page).

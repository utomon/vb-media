# Brief 2026-11-10 (Tue): One Piece Card Game

**Story:** Bandai revealed the next One Piece Card Game English roadmap on Nov 6: EB-06 East Blue (Jan 29, 2027), OP-19 "Come In, World" with an Egghead theme (Mar 5, 2027) and the new all-holo Premium Extra Booster PEB-01 "The Drawing of Your Dreams" (Apr 23, 2027).
No repeat: briefs/ Oct 27 to Nov 9 checked. Earlier One Piece briefs: EB-05 (Oct 20), OP-18 (Oct 27), ST-37/ST-38 (Nov 3). OP-19, EB-06 and PEB-01 were never briefed or posted. Only 4 days old, so it is fresh. Scheduling is Posterly (Metricool not used).

## Verified facts (official product pages + 2 independent sources)
- Announced Nov 6, 2026 (official site news list; OnePiece.gg; Polkastarter; Collector's Edge updated Nov 6).
- EB-06 EXTRA BOOSTER -EAST BLUE-: English Jan 29, 2027, $4.99 per pack, 67+1 card types; themed on the East Blue and early-story characters (official EB-06 page; Polkastarter; Collector's Edge). Japan Jan 30, 2027 (Polkastarter; Collector's Edge).
- OP-19 BOOSTER PACK -COME IN, WORLD-: pre-release Feb 26, 2027, English release Mar 5, 2027, $4.99 per pack, 126+1 card types, Egghead theme with new Leaders; tagline asks what Vegapunk will reveal (official OP-19 page; Polkastarter; Samurai Sword Tokyo; Collector's Edge). Japan Feb 27, 2027 (Polkastarter; Samurai Sword Tokyo; Collector's Edge).
- PEB-01 PREMIUM EXTRA BOOSTER -THE DRAWING OF YOUR DREAMS-: English Apr 23, 2027, all-holo, first in a new Premium Extra Booster series, 88+30 card types (Polkastarter; Collector's Edge). Japan Apr 24, 2027.
- Tournament legality: 7 days after the regional release; OP-19 cards are legal at local game stores from the pre-release date (official OP-19 page).

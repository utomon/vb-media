# Brief 2026-10-27 (Tue): One Piece Card Game

**Story:** OP-18 "The Dominance of God" hits English shelves Nov 20 ($4.99 packs, 127+1 card types), built around Water Seven and the Holy Knights of God, with Saint Gunko and Saint Shamrock debuting and Franky getting his first Leader card.
No repeat: Metricool Oct 20 to 26 (EB-05 Oct 20, MTG Star Trek, Pokémon Deck Crafter's, Gundam Stardust Trails, Pocket Dual Fusion) and briefs/ Oct 12 to 26 checked. OP-18 was never briefed or posted. EB-05 (Oct 30) and the Mihawk ban were already covered, so they are not reused.

## Verified facts
- English release Nov 20, 2026; Japan Nov 21 (official English product page; Shonen TCG citing the official EN and JP pages).
- MSRP US $4.99 per pack (official page; Shonen TCG).
- 127+1 card types in total (official page; Shonen TCG). Cards per pack not stated.
- Theme: Water Seven and the Holy Knights of God; Saint Gunko and Saint Shamrock make their card game debut (official page).
- Franky is the first OP-18 Leader, OP18-021, Green/Purple (Shonen TCG; TCGroup AU, both citing Bandai's reveal).
- Yoshitaka Amano illustrates two special cards (Shonen TCG; TCGroup AU).
- Official 4th Anniversary Event (OP-18 release event) at stores Nov 20 to Dec 17, 2026; participants get a 75-type tournament pack and P-161 Monkey.D.Luffy promo (official event page). Player sign-up via TCG+ from Nov 6.
- Tournament legal 7 days after release (official page).
